# Controls & Monitoring Framework

| Risk Area | Required Controls | Monitoring / KRI | Escalation Logic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operational / service | On-time delivery SLA, loss/damage thresholds, peak-capacity commitment, route contingency, backup carrier | On-time %, loss/damage rate, SLA breach trend, backup capacity | Escalate repeated SLA breaches or insufficient peak capacity |
| Data privacy & confidentiality | Data minimization, DPA, encryption, RBAC, logging, no secondary use, deletion / return, 24-hour incident notification | Privacy incidents, unauthorized access, late deletion exceptions | Immediate escalation for material privacy/security incident |
| Regulatory / customs | Compliance attestations, customs documentation standards, audit rights, regulatory-change notification | Customs holds, documentation errors, regulatory exceptions | Remediation plan for recurring documentation / compliance failures |
| Concentration | No single carrier >40% without senior risk acceptance | Carrier volume %, regional variance, backup capacity | Senior approval required above threshold |
| Fourth-party / subcontractor | Subcontractor inventory, prior approval, contractual flow-down, audit rights, incident reporting | Unauthorized subcontractor findings, fourth-party incidents | Remediate before scale-up if material subcontractors lack transparency |

## Governance Cadence

- Monthly KPI / KRI scorecard review
- Quarterly vendor performance review
- Annual risk reassessment
- Open-issue tracking with owner and target date
- Renew / remediate / replace decision at review gates

## Management Principle

A third-party model should be approved only when residual risk is:
1. identified,
2. supported by evidence,
3. controlled contractually and operationally,
4. accepted by the appropriate business owner, and
5. continuously monitored.
